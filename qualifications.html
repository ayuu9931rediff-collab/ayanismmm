About the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India

Established under the Chartered Accountants Act, 1949, the ICAI is the second-largest professional accounting body in the world. It sets the standard for financial reporting, auditing practice, and professional ethics across India. Preparing within this framework instills an uncompromising respect for statutory fidelity, analytical accuracy, and fiduciarily sound judgment.
